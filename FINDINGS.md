# Bug hunt — 2026-09-08

Scope: pricing engines (Dart/Python parity), the site-measurement and
final-pricing pipeline, order screens/PDFs, and add-on upgrade wiring. Read-only
sweep, three parallel agents plus direct verification of every finding below
against the actual source (file:line citations point at what was read, not
what an agent claimed).

**All six findings are now fixed** (each proven by a test that failed before
the fix and passes after, mutation-checked).

Not examined this pass: `dashboard/` write paths beyond `order-detail.ts`,
`einvoice_threshold.py`, `confirm_order.dart`, `overrides_review_screen.dart`,
`cancel_order_sheet.dart`, `revised_order_pdf.dart` layout code.

- Band selection fencepost (`band_max` exclusive) — identical in both engines.
- Deferred-material dearest-tie-break, `min_qty` estimate-only gating, and the
  RM300 per-category floor at both stages — identical in both engines.

  identical in both engines.

  absent-vs-empty-string per field, trims, and refuses stale pushes by
  `captured_at`. The bug is specifically in how the **handset** reacts to that
  refusal (finding #2), not in the merge logic itself.
- The dashboard's buyer-details correction form correctly keeps the form open
  and shows failure on any `refused_because` (`order-detail.ts:398-436`).
- `RevisedOrderData.of` correctly refuses to build a document unless
  `pricing.isComplete`, and a per_roll line still correctly refuses at final
  pricing when unmeasured — a wallpaper line can't slip through this document
  unpriced even though quote-time measurement is now optional.
